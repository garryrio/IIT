# Project Synopsis — Marga Mulya Hub

Information about Desa Marga Mulya is scattered across notice boards, chat groups and word of mouth, village data is hard to read as raw tables, local potential such as rice farming, home-based food products and bamboo crafts is rarely promoted, and residents have no structured channel to contribute updates, so a website that depends on a developer quickly becomes outdated. Marga Mulya Hub answers these problems with one platform that combines a village website, a content management system, a Village Data Hub, a potential catalogue and a community participation channel. Built with Laravel (PHP 8.3+), MySQL, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, framer-motion, Leaflet with OpenStreetMap and Chart.js, and deployed on Vercel, every piece of substantive content—profile, statistics, news, announcements, events, potentials, gallery and contacts—is stored in the database and managed by village officials without touching code. Key features include interactive charts with text and table alternatives, a layered interactive map, a smart search with filters, a monthly event calendar with calendar export, WhatsApp-ready business listings, a moderated "Citizen to Village" contribution workflow, and an admin-only income-class analysis that ranks households for fairer social assistance while publishing only aggregate figures. The site is mobile-first, written in Indonesian, and follows WCAG 2.1 AA practices. The expected impact is faster access to public information, wider market reach for local entrepreneurs and more transparent, participatory village governance, supporting SDG 9 (digital infrastructure and innovation), SDG 11 (inclusive, sustainable communities), SDG 16 (accountable and transparent institutions) and SDG 8 (decent work and local economic growth).
